# Demo listing sources

All listings were collected on 2026-11-20 from public pages run by property managers or buildings: AppFolio listing pages on the managers' own AppFolio subdomains, plus the building websites for Exhibit A2 and University Towers. No aggregators were used (no Zillow, Apartments.com, Trulia, Redfin, Craigslist or UM Off-Campus Housing). One Old Town Realty listing (406 E Jefferson) embeds a Zillow 3D Home tour in the manager's own AppFolio description, and that URL is recorded only in its notes. Each photo is the largest version available, converted to JPEG and scaled so its long edge is at most 2400px. Images narrower than 600px were skipped. Values that are not on the page are `null`.

Lat/lng come from Nominatim (OpenStreetMap), queried with a generic User-Agent at 1 request per second.

## General caveats
- Rents are what each page listed on the access date, for the whole unit (`rentBasis: total`). Most student houses are for the 2027-28 lease year.
- Campus Realty pages say "Furniture in photo may not represent the provided set."
- AppFolio photos have no captions, so `caption` is `null`. Exhibit A2 captions are the site's alt text. University Towers captions give the unit number taken from the image filename.
- Matterport cross-checks: the University Towers Apt G tours (units 802 and 1015) are the same physical units as the gallery photos. The Exhibit A2 tour is unit #506, and its gallery is for the same floorplan; the photos are probably of that unit, but the page does not say so.
